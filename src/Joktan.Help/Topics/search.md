# Search

The search box in the top bar finds things in your [notes](help:notes), in help, and in your
[messages and alerts](help:alerts-and-messages). Start typing and the results appear below it.

A result has every word you typed, in any order and ignoring case. Each one shows where it was found
and the line with the most of your words, with your words marked. The marks carry over to the note, help
topic, message or alert you open, which scrolls to the first of them.

## Going to a result

Click a result, or use **Up** and **Down** and press **Enter**.

- **A note** opens on the Notes page.
- **A help topic** opens in the help window.
- **A message or alert** opens its panel in the top bar, scrolled to it and marked. If the Alerts panel
  was showing only another level, it goes back to showing all levels.

The **×** in the box clears your words, the results and the marks. **Escape** hides the results, and
pressing it again clears the box too. Your words stay in the box after opening a result, so you can click
it to go back to the results.

## Choosing what to search

On the Settings / Search page, each area has a switch: **Notes**, **Help**, **Messages** and **Alerts**.
Changes apply at once and are remembered. The box's hint names the areas that are switched on.

Switching an area off only leaves it out of the results. Nothing in it changes.

To change the colour of the marks, set **Highlight** and **Text on the highlight** on the Settings /
Colours page. See [Themes](help:themes).

## Good to know

- Notes are searched as you wrote them, so a word inside a link's address is found too.
- Only the latest 200 messages and 200 alerts are kept, so older ones cannot be found.
- Passwords are never searched.
